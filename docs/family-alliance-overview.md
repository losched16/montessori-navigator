# Montessori Family Alliance

**Powered by The Montessori Foundation**

A trusted home for Montessori families and the schools that guide them — an AI-powered platform that strengthens the bridge between the classroom and the home, built in partnership with Montessori schools, never to replace them.

---

## Why It Exists

Montessori schools share the same hard truth: **families who don't understand Montessori don't see its value, and families who don't see its value don't re-enroll.** Schools spend enormous time fielding the same parent questions over and over — at 10pm, on weekends, between work and pickup. Teachers can't be everywhere. Parents Google, find conflicting answers, and arrive at conferences with ideas that contradict what the classroom teaches.

The Montessori Family Alliance gives every family in your school a 24/7, philosophy-aligned resource that reinforces what their child experiences in your environment — so families come to conferences fluent in Montessori, retention goes up, and your teachers can focus on what they do best.

## How It Works for Schools

### Subscription
- **$12 per family, per year** — billed annually
- **10-family minimum** ($120/year minimum)
- **14-day free trial** — full access, card required up front, cancel anytime
- Pay once for the school; every family you invite gets full access at no cost to them

### Setup (15 minutes)
1. Subscribe at [familyalliance.montessori.org/for-schools/pricing](https://familyalliance.montessori.org/for-schools/pricing)
2. Stripe handles the trial + billing
3. Create your admin account (email confirmed automatically)
4. Land on your school dashboard

### School Admin Dashboard

**📊 Overview**  
At-a-glance view of how many families are enrolled, how many invitations are pending, and engagement.

**👨‍👩‍👧 Families**  
See every family that's joined your school — when they signed up, who's in the household.

**✉️ Invitations**  
Two ways to bring families on board:
- **Share a link** — Copy your school's join URL and paste it into your existing parent communications (newsletter, welcome packet, parent portal)
- **Bulk invite by email** — Paste a list of emails (CSV or one per line) and we send a branded invitation to each family on your behalf

**👤 Admins & Staff**  
Add other admins to help manage. Anyone you invite gets full admin access. They sign up with the email you invited them with — no separate account creation.

**⚙️ School Settings**  
- School profile (name, address, website, phone, credentials — AMI / AMS / MACTE / IMC / Other)
- **Subscription management** via Stripe portal: update payment, change family count, view invoices, cancel anytime

### What School Admins See

- Who has signed up
- Pending invitations and their status (sent, accepted, expired, revoked)
- Total seats used vs. seats available
- Trial end date or next billing date

### Co-Branded for Your School

When a family clicks your invitation link, they see **your school's name** and credentials on the signup page. The platform is unmistakably "their school's resource."

## What Your Families Get

When you invite a family, they sign up for free (covered by your subscription) and get access to **everything** the platform offers:

### 💬 Abigail — The AI Montessori Guide
A philosophy-aligned AI assistant trained on the Montessori Foundation's teachings. Parents ask:
- *"My toddler is biting other kids — what do I do?"*
- *"Why does my child carry a single Lego around for hours?"*
- *"How do I prepare for our first parent-teacher conference?"*

Abigail answers in plain language, **grounded in Foundation philosophy**, and **never contradicts what the classroom teaches**. She knows the child's age, sensitive periods, and the family's communication preferences.

### 📚 Foundation Library
**495+ articles, videos, and webinars** from Tim Seldin and Montessori Foundation contributors — searchable and filterable by topic, age group, or curriculum area.

### 📖 Tomorrow's Child
The Foundation's flagship newsletter for parents and educators. **43 archived issues** spanning 2010–2026, available to read or download as PDFs. New issues added as they're released.

### 👶 Baby Milestones (0–36 Months)
Month-by-month development guides covering gross motor, hand development, communication, and Montessori activities at each stage. Parents see what's age-appropriate and how to support their child's growth.

### ✨ Child Development & Curriculum Tracking
- **2,566 skills** from the official Montessori Scope & Sequence
- 10 curriculum areas: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics, Geography, History, Cosmic Studies, Cultural, Movement, and Creative Arts
- Per-child progress tracking — what they've mastered, what's emerging, what's next

### 📋 At-Home Learning Plans
AI-generated plans aligned with the curriculum framework. Parents say *"My 4-year-old loves pouring activities and is starting to recognize letters,"* and Abigail builds a week of activities that **complement** the classroom — never duplicating or overriding what the school teaches.

### 📊 Progress Reports
Parents can generate written progress reports for their child — useful for conferences, for sharing with grandparents, or for keeping a personal record of growth over time. Builds the same vocabulary teachers use.

### 🏡 Home Environment & Room Vision
- Room-by-room guides: entryway, kitchen, bedroom, playroom, outdoor space
- AI-powered "Room Vision": parents upload a photo of a room and get a Montessori-aligned redesign suggestion
- Helps parents create a prepared environment at home that mirrors the school's principles

### 📓 Daily Observations
Parents log small moments — *"Eli concentrated on the pink tower for 22 minutes today"* — and build a journal of growth they bring to conferences.

### 🤝 School Evaluation Tool
For families considering your school OR considering moving: a structured tour-debrief that helps them recognize authentic Montessori practice. (When they're already enrolled with you, this becomes a way to articulate why your school stands out.)

### 💭 Memories & Notes
Save important responses from Abigail, attach notes to specific children, build a private journal.

## How It Helps Your School

### Re-enrollment
Families who understand Montessori — who see their child's growth, who know why work cycles matter, who recognize sensitive periods when they appear — **don't leave**. Family Alliance is the year-round retention layer your school has been building manually.

### Onboarding
New families spend their first months confused. The Alliance gives them **the same vocabulary your teachers use**, structured age-appropriate guidance, and a place to bring their late-night questions instead of your head of school's inbox.

### Teacher Workload
Stop being the only Montessori interpreter for your families. Abigail answers the questions that don't actually require teacher time — so when teachers DO meet with parents, the conversation can be substantive.

### Marketing
Prospective families who tour your school and ask *"How do you support parents?"* now have a concrete answer: *"Every family at our school gets the Montessori Family Alliance — Tim Seldin's AI guide, the Foundation library, child development tools — covered by our school."* That's a recruiting differentiator.

### Conference Quality
Parents arrive at conferences having logged their own observations, having read up on their child's sensitive periods, having generated their own progress notes. **Your conferences become substantive partnerships, not Montessori 101 lectures.**

## Privacy & Data

- Each family's observations, child profiles, and notes are **private to that family** — not visible to the school admin, not visible to other families
- The school admin can see **enrollment** (who's signed up) but not **content** (what they've logged or asked Abigail)
- All data is stored in a SOC2-compliant Supabase Postgres database with row-level security
- Stripe handles all payment data — we never store card numbers
- Families can export or delete all their data from Settings at any time
